The **Sri Lanka Investment Forum 2026** is a premier platform bringing together investors, policymakers, business leaders, and development partners to explore the next chapter of Sri Lanka's growth story.

Set against a backdrop of economic recovery, reform, and renewed momentum, the forum is designed to spotlight investment-ready opportunities, high-growth sectors, and strategic partnerships that can shape the country's future.

## Event Details

- **Date:** March 30, 2026
- **Venue:** Cinnamon Life, Colombo
- **Format:** One-day international investment forum
- **Audience:** Investors, corporate leaders, public sector decision-makers, and development stakeholders

## Why Attend

The forum offers a focused opportunity to:

- discover investment-ready projects aligned with national development priorities
- engage directly with policymakers and institutional leaders
- gain insight into Sri Lanka's economic outlook and reform agenda
- build relationships through strategic networking and B2B engagement
- explore partnerships that support sustainable and inclusive growth

## What to Expect

Attendees can expect a dynamic programme built around:

- investment opportunities across priority sectors
- policy and leadership dialogue
- market intelligence and reform updates
- public-private partnership opportunities
- curated investor networking

The event is structured to create both visibility and access: visibility into Sri Lanka's most promising sectors, and access to the people and institutions shaping them.

## Programme Highlights

The forum programme includes:

- registration and inaugural session
- Sri Lanka's economic recovery outlook
- Sri Lanka's investment ecosystem
- shaping the green future
- EU-Sri Lanka investor dialogue
- deal matching and B2B connect sessions

## Featured Sector Themes

Key sector conversations are expected to include:

- logistics, integrated warehousing, and aviation services
- value-added processing, pharmaceuticals, and industrial manufacturing
- nautical and waterfront tourism, hotel development, and theme parks
- technology and innovation parks, university towns, and film production hubs
